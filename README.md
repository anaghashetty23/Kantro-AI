KANTRO AI

Your Break. Your Food. Your Time.

An AI-Powered College Canteen Pre-Ordering & Kitchen Coordination Platform

KANTRO AI is an intelligent canteen management platform designed to simplify food ordering for college students while improving kitchen coordination and operational efficiency.

The platform allows students to explore menus, plan orders around their academic breaks, receive AI-powered food recommendations, and select convenient pickup times. At the same time, kitchen employees can manage incoming orders, while administrators oversee canteen operations through a centralized dashboard.

---

Overview

In a typical college canteen, students often spend a significant portion of their limited break time waiting in queues. During peak hours, kitchen staff also face difficulties managing multiple orders and coordinating preparation schedules.

KANTRO AI addresses these challenges through an integrated pre-ordering and kitchen management system.

Instead of ordering only after reaching the canteen, students can place orders in advance and collect their food at a suitable time.

Our goal is simple: Make every college break more convenient, organized, and productive.

How It Works

The platform connects three different user roles through one integrated system.

User Role| Primary Responsibility
Student| Explore menus, interact with AI, place pre-orders and track orders
Kitchen Employee| Receive orders, manage preparation and update order status
Kitchen Administrator| Manage canteen operations, monitor orders and oversee resources

Order Workflow

1. Student logs in using their college credentials.
2. Student enters their break time or interacts with the AI assistant.
3. Student explores the menu and selects food items.
4. The system checks availability and pickup feasibility.
5. Student confirms the order and selects a payment method.
6. The kitchen employee receives the order.
7. Food is prepared and the order status is updated.
8. Student collects the order at the canteen counter.

System Architecture

KANTRO AI follows a client-server architecture with three primary layers.

Presentation Layer

- React
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS

Application Layer

- Node.js
- Express.js
- Order processing
- Authentication
- Scheduling and capacity validation
- Gemini API integration

Data Layer

- SQLite
- Student records
- Menu information
- Orders and order items
- Kitchen information
- Inventory and payment records

The frontend communicates with the backend through APIs, while the backend handles business logic and database operations.
